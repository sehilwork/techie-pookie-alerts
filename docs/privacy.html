Privacy policy

Techie Pookie Alerts is used only by the owner of the Techie Pookie YouTube channel, for that channel.

  • What it reads: the channel's own subscriber totals (subscribers gained and lost), using read-only YouTube Analytics access granted by the owner.
  • What it does with it: sends the owner a Telegram message with the count. Counts are kept in this public repository's state file.
  • What it does not do: it reads no data about viewers or other users, posts nothing, and shares nothing with anyone else.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party connections.
